## Open Positions

The Frank lab is hiring enthusiastic and talented post-docs, graduate students, and undergrads. Prospective post-docs should send a CV, statement of research interests, and three references to Dr. Frank ([mhf47@cornell.edu](mailto:mhf47@cornell.edu)). Graduate students can apply through the [Cornell Graduate School](https://gradschool.cornell.edu/academics/fields-of-study/subject/plant-biology/plant-biology-phd-ithaca/). Prospective undergrads can send Dr. Frank an email with their resume, a brief statement about their interests in plant science and molecular biology, and two references.
